Common questions

How do I delete my account?
Open the app, go to Settings, and tap "Delete account" near the bottom. This permanently deletes your account and data and cannot be undone.

How do I invite a co-parent or nanny to a baby's data?
In Settings, open the baby you want to share and tap "Share with a partner" to generate an invite code. Share that code with the person you want to invite — they can enter it from the sign-in or Settings screen to get access.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID, not inside the app. On your iPhone, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions to view, change, or cancel your Barnið Premium subscription.
