Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This policy explains what information Barnið ("the app") collects, why, where it goes, how long it's kept and what your rights are. Barnið is a baby-tracking app that helps parents and other caregivers log and share a baby's sleep, feeds, nappy changes, appointments, and the medicines and vitamins they're given.

In short

  • Barnið stores health information about your child (and, if you use "Not born yet", about the pregnancy). We only do that with your explicit consent, which you can withdraw in Settings.
  • Your child's records are shared with the other guardians you invite. Apart from them, only we, to run the service as described below, and the service providers listed below can access them. They're never used for advertising and never sold.
  • The data is stored on servers in Ireland (EU), with encrypted backups in the EU.
  • We use anonymous product analytics that never include names, notes, dates or medicine details.
  • You can delete your account and data in the app at any time.

Who is responsible for your data

The controller of your personal data is Eggert Már, an individual in Iceland who develops and runs Barnið. Contact: info@eggertmar.com. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Eggert Már. If Barnið is moved to a company, we'll update this policy and tell you in the app.

Who this policy is about

Barnið is used by adults (parents, guardians and other caregivers) to record care information about a baby. It isn't directed at children and offers no account or interface for a child. This policy covers you as an account holder, the other guardians you share a baby with, and the baby whose information you record.

What we collect

Your account

  • Sign-in: when you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive an identifier from that provider and, if you allow it, your name and email address (with Apple, possibly a private relay address).
  • Profile: the display name you choose, which the other guardians of your baby see next to the entries you log, and your app language.
  • Consent record: when you give or withdraw consent for health information, we record that you did, when, which version of the consent text you saw, in which language, and your app version.

Your baby's profile

  • Name (or a placeholder until you choose one), birth date, and an optional photo. Photos are re-encoded on your phone before upload, which removes location and other metadata.

Health information about your child

This is special-category data under the GDPR, and we treat all of it as health information:

  • Care logs: feeds (breast, bottle or pump, side, amount, times), sleep (times, nap or night), nappy changes (kind, rash) and appointments (title, time).
  • Medicines and vitamins: if you use this feature, the medicines and supplements you add (name, form, dose amount, schedule, course dates, minimum gap between doses) and each dose logged as given or skipped, with its time and which guardian logged it. The app doesn't calculate, recommend or check doses; it only records what you enter. Nothing about medicines is stored unless you add one.
  • Notes: any free text you add to an entry.

Pregnancy information

  • If you add a baby that's "Not born yet": that status, the due date, appointments you add during the pregnancy, and when the birth is recorded. This is health information about the pregnancy.
  • Contraction timer (Premium): if you use it, the start and end time of each contraction you time. The app shows how long each contraction lasted and the time since the previous one started, calculated from those times. It doesn't tell you whether labour has started or when to go to hospital; it only records. Contraction timings are information about the health of the person who is pregnant. If that isn't you, please log them only with their agreement.
  • Hospital-bag list: the items on your packing list for the birth, whether each is packed, who ticked it and when. You can add your own items; please don't write health details in them that you don't want the other guardians to see.

Sharing between guardians

  • Which accounts are guardians of which baby, and the invite codes used to join (who created and redeemed each code, and when).
  • Live status: while a guardian has a feed or sleep timer running, the other guardians of that baby see it live (their display name, what's running and since when). This is sent through our backend while it's happening and isn't stored as a separate record.

Subscriptions

  • When you buy Barnið Premium, Apple handles the payment. We receive and store the product, the purchase and renewal dates, whether it auto-renews, whether it was refunded, Apple's transaction identifier, which baby the subscription is for, and which account bought it. We never see your payment card.
  • To link a purchase to the right baby, the app passes the baby's internal ID (a random code, not the name) to Apple with the purchase.

Usage analytics

We use Aptabase, a privacy-focused analytics service, hosted in its EU region, to see which features are used and to catch bugs. The app sends events such as: a session started; a screen was viewed; a feed, sleep, nappy change or appointment was logged (with only its category, such as "bottle" or "nap", and whether it was logged live or afterwards); a sign-in method was chosen; an invite code was created or redeemed, or an invite link was opened (with only whether the person was signed in); a baby was created; a baby that's not born yet was added, a birth was recorded or a pregnancy was removed (with no dates); the hospital-bag list was opened (and whether this created the default list), an item was ticked (and whether it was one of the default items) or an item was added (never what the item is); a suggestion to invite your partner or to log a first entry was shown, tapped or closed (for the first-entry suggestion, only whether a feed, sleep or nappy was chosen); a first-day reminder was scheduled or opened; a paywall or offer was shown; a purchase started, succeeded or failed (with the plan); a sync error happened (with a general category only); and the health consent screen was shown, or consent was given, declined or withdrawn (with no other details).

With each event, Aptabase also receives your device's model, operating-system version, app version and language, and a session ID that changes regularly. Aptabase may derive an approximate country from your internet connection. Our backend also sends Aptabase one event for each subscription notification Apple sends us (for example a renewal, a billing failure or a refund), with the plan, price, currency, App Store country and offer type.

Analytics events never include your name or email, your baby's name, your user or baby ID, notes, medicine names or doses, dates of birth or due dates, invite codes, or amounts you log. They aren't linked to your account, so we generally can't find a particular person's events. They're used only by us, never for advertising, and never shared or sold. Barnið doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites, so the app doesn't show Apple's tracking prompt.

Technical logs

  • Our backend provider keeps short-term logs of requests to our servers (for example time, the requested address, your account ID and your IP address) for security and troubleshooting.
  • When you visit our website or these pages, the hosting provider receives standard request data, such as your IP address, your browser and the page you asked for.

Support email

  • If you email us, we have your email address and whatever you write, which might include information about your child. Please don't send more than we need to help you.

Kept only on your phone

  • Medicine reminder settings and quiet hours, notification settings, and Live Activities are handled on your phone. Reminders are local notifications; they aren't sent through our servers.
  • Reminders and Live Activities can show your baby's name, photo and a medicine's name on your lock screen.
  • Sleep Time estimates and month cards are calculated in the app from your logs.

Your consent for health information

  • When: before Barnið stores any health information, every guardian (the one who adds a baby and everyone who joins with an invite code) is asked separately, on a screen of its own, to consent. The screen sums up what's stored and why, with more details on the same screen. You consent by tapping "Agree and continue" directly under a sentence that says you agree to the app storing and using health information about the children, and confirms that you're their parent or guardian or care for them with permission. Nothing is selected in advance, and "I don't agree" is just as easy to choose. Accepting the Terms of Service isn't consent to this. If you already used Barnið before this was introduced, you're asked the next time you open the app. If you used Barnið before the consent screen arrived and haven't opened the app since, your data stays as it is until you open the app and answer the question, or delete your account.
  • If you don't consent: Barnið can't store or show your child's care information. If you're new, nothing is saved. If you used Barnið before, what's already stored is kept unchanged for 30 days while you decide; after that, babies only you have access to are deleted, and you're removed from babies you share (their records stay with the other guardians).
  • Withdrawing: you can withdraw your consent at any time in Settings. Babies only you have access to are then deleted from our servers with all their records and photos, you leave the babies you share with others (their records, including what you logged, stay with the other guardians), and the app removes the records from your phone. Your account itself stays until you delete it. Withdrawing doesn't affect what was done before you withdrew.
  • For a child: a child can't consent, so a parent or guardian consents on the child's behalf. When you consent, you confirm that you're the child's parent or legal guardian, or that you care for the child with their permission.

Who can see your information

Other guardians you invite

A baby's profile and all its records, including medicines, doses and pregnancy information (such as contraction timings and the hospital-bag list), are visible to every guardian who has joined that baby with an invite code, and they see who logged each entry. Sharing a baby's record between caregivers is the core of the app.

The app doesn't yet let you remove another guardian or leave a shared baby yourself. Email us and we'll do it for you.

People you choose to send something to

If you use Share report (for example, to send a summary to a doctor or nurse), share a month card, or share an invite (a link with the invite code in it), it goes through your phone's share sheet to whoever you choose. We don't get a copy. On a phone without Barnið, the invite link opens a page on our website. You can leave medicines out of a report with its "Include medicine and vitamins" switch.

The operator (us)

We run an internal admin tool to operate Barnið. For each account it shows the email, display name, how the account signs in (Apple or Google), sign-up and last-activity dates, the account's babies (name, birth date, Premium status and subscriptions), and a list of the entries logged for those babies: the type and time of each feed (including kind, side and amount), sleep (nap or night), nappy change (including rash), and medicine dose (medicine or vitamin, given or skipped), and which guardian logged it. It doesn't show notes, medicine names, dose amounts or photos. We use it for support, to grant free Premium, and to understand how the app is used. As the operator we can also technically access the database directly; we do that only to maintain the service, fix problems or answer your requests.

How long we keep it, and deletion

This is what happens today. Where we haven't finished building automatic deletion yet, we say so, and we say what we're working towards. When that changes, we'll update this policy. Deletions that happen after a set time are made by a job that runs once a day, so they can take up to a day longer than stated here.

  • Your account and your babies' records are kept while your account exists. We don't yet delete inactive accounts automatically. We're working towards deleting accounts that haven't been used for 24 months, after warning you by email first where we have an address.
  • Deleting your account (Settings → Delete account) permanently deletes, at once, your sign-in record, your profile, and every baby where you are the only guardian, with all of that baby's records. Babies you share with other guardians are kept for them, and you're removed from them. The entries you logged on those babies stay with the baby, but from then on they're shown under the name of the remaining guardian who joined the baby first. Invite codes you created and your consent record are deleted with your account.
  • Photos: a baby's photo is deleted from storage within a day of the baby being permanently deleted from our servers (see below for when that happens). When you replace a photo, the app deletes the old one.
  • Deleting an entry or a medicine removes it from the app on every guardian's phone. On our servers, everything in it is erased at once: what you typed or measured, its times and the options you picked. All that's left is a bare record that an entry was deleted: which baby it was for, when it was deleted, and who added it. That's kept so every guardian's phone learns about the deletion, and permanently deleted 90 days later. Deleting a medicine deletes its dose history the same way.
  • Deleting a baby removes it from the app on every guardian's phone. On our servers the baby and all its records are kept unchanged for 30 days and then permanently deleted, with its photo. A baby that no guardian has access to any more is permanently deleted in the same way, 30 days after it last changed. If you want a baby removed sooner, email us and we'll do it.
  • Removing a pregnancy permanently deletes the baby that's "Not born yet" and everything recorded for it, including appointments and contraction timings, from our servers at once, and any photo within a day. If you remove it in a version of the app older than 1.4.0, it's deleted 30 days later, like a deleted baby.
  • Deletion markers: when we permanently delete a baby or an entry, we keep a marker so that an old copy on a phone that has been offline can't put it back. A marker holds only a random ID number, the kind of record and when it was deleted, nothing you entered. Markers for a baby's entries are deleted with the baby; the marker for a deleted baby is kept.
  • Backups: so we can recover from a technical failure, we back up the database every 30 minutes. Each backup is made on a computer we run ourselves in Iceland, encrypted there with a key only we hold, and stored with Cloudflare in the EU. Each backup is deleted automatically after at most 30 days, so anything deleted from our servers, whether you deleted it or it was deleted under the rules above, is gone from the backups no more than 30 days later. Photos aren't backed up. We use backups only to recover from a failure and to test that recovery works.
  • Invite codes are valid for 24 hours. A code is deleted 7 days after it expires or is used, or earlier if the baby is deleted or the guardian who created it deletes their account.
  • Your consent record is kept while your account exists and is deleted with it.
  • Technical logs of requests to our servers are deleted automatically by Supabase, never later than 90 days after they're made.
  • Analytics events are kept by Aptabase for up to 5 years. They aren't linked to you.
  • Support emails are kept for as long as we need them to help you, and deleted 12 months after the last message unless we need them for a legal claim.
  • On your phone: the app keeps a copy of your babies' records so it works offline. Deleting your account erases it. Signing out currently doesn't; delete the app to remove it. Your phone's own backups (for example iCloud) may contain a copy, under your control.
  • What you've shared (reports, month cards) is outside our control once sent.
  • Subscription records are kept with the baby they belong to. If the guardian who bought a subscription deletes their account, the record stays with the baby without the link to them. Apple keeps its own records of your purchases.
  • Contraction timings are kept while the baby is "Not born yet". When the birth is recorded, the timer is hidden, and the contraction timings are permanently deleted from our servers 30 days later. Removing the pregnancy, deleting the baby, or deleting your account (if you're the baby's only guardian) removes them in the same way as the baby's other records.
  • The hospital-bag list is kept while the baby is "Not born yet" and permanently deleted from our servers 30 days after the birth is recorded. Removing the pregnancy, deleting the baby, or deleting your account (if you're the baby's only guardian) removes it in the same way as the baby's other records. A ticked item keeps no record of who ticked it once that person deletes their account.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • have inaccurate data corrected (most of it you can edit in the app);
  • have your data erased (you can delete entries, medicines, babies and your account in the app);
  • restrict our processing in some circumstances;
  • receive the data you gave us in a machine-readable format and have it sent to another service (portability);
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests, including analytics;
  • withdraw your consent for health information at any time in Settings, without affecting what was done before.

As a parent or guardian you can exercise these rights for your child. To exercise any of them, email info@eggertmar.com from the address linked to your account, or tell us how to recognise your account. We'll answer within one month. Because analytics events aren't linked to you, we usually can't find or delete a particular person's events.

You also have the right to complain to the Icelandic Data Protection Authority, Persónuvernd: Laugavegur 166, 105 Reykjavík, Iceland; postur@personuvernd.is; phone +354 510 9600; www.personuvernd.is. If you live elsewhere in the EEA, you can also complain to your local authority. We'd appreciate the chance to sort things out with you first.

Security

All connections between the app and our servers are encrypted (HTTPS). Data on our servers is encrypted at rest by our hosting provider. Backups are encrypted before they're stored, with a key only we hold. Access rules in the database let each account read and change only the babies it's a guardian of. On your phone, the app's data is protected by iOS data protection when your phone is locked. If a personal data breach happens that's likely to put you at risk, we'll tell Persónuvernd within 72 hours of finding out and, where the risk is high, tell you.

Children's privacy

Barnið isn't directed at children and isn't meant to be used by them. It's a tool for adults to record care information about a child in their care, and information about the child is entered and controlled by the child's guardians. We don't knowingly collect information directly from children.

If we make material changes, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app. If a change affects what you consented to, we'll ask for your consent again.
