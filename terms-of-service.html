What Barnið is

Barnið helps parents and other caregivers log and share a baby's sleep, feeding, nappy changes, appointments, and the medicines and vitamins they are given, and, before the birth, pregnancy appointments and contraction timings. It is a caregiving/organizational tool, not a medical device, and does not provide medical advice. Predictions and suggestions the app makes (such as a predicted next sleep window) are estimates based on the information you enter and general age-based patterns — they are not medical guidance and should never replace the judgment of a pediatrician or other qualified professional.

Medicines and vitamins

Schedules, minimum gaps between doses, reminders and "next dose" information are based solely on what you enter and do not replace the product label or the advice of a doctor, pharmacist or other qualified professional.

  • Entries can take time to reach other guardians, and may not reach them at all while a phone is offline or if saving to our servers fails. Before giving a dose, check with the other caregivers directly; do not rely on the app alone to know whether a dose has already been given. When an entry you logged has not synced, you are responsible for letting the other caregivers know.

Pregnancy and the contraction timer

  • The contraction timer only records when each contraction you time starts and ends, and shows the length of each contraction and the time since the previous one started. It does not tell you whether labour has started, how it is progressing, or when to go to hospital or call your midwife, and it does not monitor you or your baby.
  • Follow the advice of your midwife, maternity unit or doctor about when to get in touch. If you are worried about anything, contact them straight away, whatever the app shows. In an emergency, call 112.
  • Timings can be wrong if a contraction is started or stopped late, and may take time to reach the other guardians, or not reach them while a phone is offline. Don't rely on the app alone.
