What Barnið is

Barnið helps parents and other caregivers log and share a baby's sleep, feeding, nappy changes, appointments, and the medicines and vitamins they are given. It is a caregiving/organizational tool, not a medical device, and does not provide medical advice. Predictions and suggestions the app makes (such as a predicted next sleep window) are estimates based on the information you enter and general age-based patterns — they are not medical guidance and should never replace the judgment of a pediatrician or other qualified professional.
